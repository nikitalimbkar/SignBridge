Step 6 — webcam_prediction.py
It loads the trained model:
sign_model.pkl

webcam_prediction.py uses the trained model to recognize the sign in real time from the webcam.

First, create_dataset.py extracts hand landmarks from the dataset and creates a CSV. Then
train_model.py uses that CSV to train the Random Forest model and saves it as sign_model.pkl.
Finally, webcam_prediction.py takes live webcam input, extracts the same landmarks, gives them to
the trained model, and predicts the sign
